How to play
  1. Create a game and send the link (or code) to your opponent.
  2. Place your nine ships on your grid. Ships can't overlap or touch, not even diagonally.
  3. Take turns firing at the enemy grid. You'll learn whether each shot hit or missed. A hit lets you fire again; your turn ends when you miss.
  4. When every square of a ship is hit, it sinks and the water around it is marked for you. Sink the whole fleet to win.
